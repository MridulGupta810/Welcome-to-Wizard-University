The School of Spellcraft

The School of Spellcraft stands as the oldest and most prestigious faculty within Wizard University of Arcania. For over eight centuries, students have gathered within its enchanted halls to study the foundations of magical theory, master powerful spells, and unlock the secrets of the arcane arts. Under the guidance of distinguished professors and master spellcasters, students learn everything from basic enchantments to advanced elemental manipulation. Combining ancient wisdom with modern magical innovation, the School of Spellcraft prepares future generations of witches and wizards to become leaders, scholars, and guardians of magical knowledge.

  1. Bachelor of Spellcasting
    Master the fundamentals of magical theory and practical spellcasting.
  2. Advanced Combat Magic
    Defensive and offensive magical techniques.
  3. Elemental Manipulation
    Control fire, water, earth, air, and lightning.
  4. Ancient Runes & Enchantments
    Study magical symbols and powerful enchantments.
