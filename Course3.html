School of Magical Creatures

The School of Magical Creatures is dedicated to the study, protection, and understanding of the world's most extraordinary beings. For centuries, students and scholars have worked alongside dragons, phoenixes, unicorns, griffins, and countless other magical species to deepen humanity's understanding of the enchanted world. Under the guidance of experienced creature specialists, students learn the principles of magical creature behavior, conservation, communication, and care. The School is home to vast creature sanctuaries and research facilities where students gain practical experience working with some of the rarest and most fascinating creatures in existence.

  1. Dragon Studies
    Behavior, communication, and care of dragons.
  2. Mythical Creature Conservation
    Protect endangered magical species.
  3. Phoenix Biology
    Study the life cycle of phoenixes.
  4. Beast Taming & Management
    Train and work alongside magical creatures.
