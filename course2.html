School of Alchemy

The School of Alchemy is one of the most respected faculties at Wizard University of Arcania, renowned for centuries of groundbreaking discoveries in potion-making, magical chemistry, and restorative magic. Since its establishment, the School has trained generations of alchemists, healers, and magical researchers whose innovations have transformed the enchanted world. Students explore the delicate balance between science and magic, learning to create powerful elixirs, healing remedies, enchanted substances, and experimental magical compounds. Guided by experienced professors and surrounded by state-of-the-art alchemical laboratories, aspiring alchemists develop both technical expertise and creative thinking.
